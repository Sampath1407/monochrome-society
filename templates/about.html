Monochrome Society is a digital gallery built around one simple idea: art has no single interpretation.

THE IDEA

We don't tell you what to see.

Most galleries tell you what an artwork means before you even have the chance to decide for yourself.

Monochrome Society does the opposite. We show you the artwork and leave the meaning open.

Look at a piece. Think about it. Make up a story. Then see what everyone else saw.

01

LOOK

Take your time. There is no correct way to experience an artwork.

02

INTERPRET

Your imagination becomes part of the artwork's story.

03

SHARE

Tell us what you saw. Someone else's interpretation might completely change yours.
